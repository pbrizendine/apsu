This notice describes the information used by this version of APSU, a text roleplay service.

Information we use

  • Account: email, username, authentication credentials handled by Supabase, account creation date, and a record of agreeing to the terms.
  • Age eligibility: date of birth, stored privately to apply the 13+ account limit and 18+ world restrictions. It is not part of player or character profiles.
  • Your contributions: player and character profiles, uploaded pictures and banners, rooms, items, messages and forum content if that feature becomes available.
  • Play state: character location, inventory, world membership, live presence, and temporary typing activity.
  • Safety and administration: reports, help requests, warnings, bans, role assignments, moderation history and privacy requests.
  • Technical information: hosting and authentication providers receive network information such as IP addresses and request metadata to operate and protect the service.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide accounts, roleplay, communication, age restrictions, moderation, security and request handling. Depending on applicable law, these uses may rely on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in protecting it, legal obligations, or consent where required. Optional browser preferences use your storage choice.

Who can see it

Other signed-in players can see your APSU profile, username, and the character or world information made available through play. Email and date of birth are not public profile fields. IC chat is shared with the people present when it is sent; OOC chat reaches the connected world audience. Whispers and item-show notices go to their intended participants. Direct messages are available to their two participants and are not end-to-end encrypted.

World admins can access reports and moderation records for their world. APSU staff and authorized infrastructure operators may access data needed to operate the service and respond to safety or legal issues. Only APSU staff can review privacy requests.

Service providers

APSU uses Supabase for accounts, database, file storage and live updates, and Cloudflare for hosting and network delivery. These providers may process information outside your country. This build includes the Supabase browser library locally and does not contact Google Fonts or load advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

Imagine (optional)

If you generate a scene, the prompt you review and your own OpenAI API key pass through our Cloudflare relay to OpenAI. APSU does not store the key, prompt, or generated image. The image remains in your page until closed unless you download it. OpenAI applies its own data and billing policies. This initial feature is limited to adult accounts.

Single-player worlds

Solo saves, messages, character memory, and progress remain in this browser until you delete them or clear site storage; they do not use the multiplayer 24-hour expiry. Exported save files remain under your control. Optional solo AI dialogue sends selected scene context and your API key through APSU’s relay to your chosen service: OpenAI, or OpenRouter and its selected model provider. The key is held in tab memory by default. If you enable Remember this connection, the dialogue key and selected model are stored without encryption in this browser until you disconnect or clear site storage. Credentials are excluded from world saves. APSU does not persist relay request bodies; the selected service and model provider apply their own data and billing policies. OpenRouter requests exclude providers that collect data and respect your account privacy settings. Connecting a key requires adult self-attestation, which is not verified age identification. Signing in does not upload your solo world.

Retention

IC, OOC and direct message access expires after 24 hours. Scheduled cleanup removes expired records; physical deletion depends on the cleanup job running. Downloaded chat logs are permanent files under the downloader’s control, and other users may keep screenshots or copies. Account, profile, world, inventory and moderation records persist until removed through the relevant controls or a reviewed request. A final retention schedule for operational logs, backups and moderation records is still required before wider release.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile, manage uploaded content, choose browser storage preferences, block direct messages from another player, and submit an access, correction or deletion request through Privacy & deletion requests. Requests are reviewed manually; the page does not instantly erase an account. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to a supervisory authority.

Deletion may require retaining limited records for security, legal obligations or unresolved disputes. We will explain any applicable limit when responding. We cannot delete copies other players have downloaded. A public contact route for people who cannot sign in has not yet been configured.

Young people

APSU is for people aged 13 and older. Registration screens out under-13 dates before creating an account, and the database validates eligibility. Players aged 13–17 cannot enter worlds marked 18+. Date-of-birth declarations are not proof of identity or independently verified age. Do not submit an under-13 child’s information. Age-assurance and parental-permission requirements vary by jurisdiction and must be reviewed before broader availability.

Changes

We will date material changes to this notice. New optional uses of information require appropriate notice and, where required, a separate choice.
